The full game

World 1, Thornvale Meadows, is free. One in-app purchase, Full Realm, unlocks the other five worlds, their heroes, every armory unlock, local multiplayer and Heroic mode. It is a one-time purchase with no ads and no subscription.

Restoring your purchase: on a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings in the game (or the Full game screen) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to steer (on Mac and keyboards: W A S D or the arrow keys).
  • Leave your land and a trail follows you. Ride back into your land to claim everything inside the loop.
  • If a rival touches your trail, you are knocked out. Touch theirs to knock them out.
  • Surround a rival's castle to take all of their land and coins.
  • Most coins when time runs out wins. In Elimination, be the last kingdom left.

Local multiplayer

Two devices on the same Wi-Fi, both with the full game. One taps Host a game, the other Join a game. If the other device doesn't appear, check that both allowed Local Network access when asked (iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).
