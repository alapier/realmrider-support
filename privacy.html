Realmrider Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Realmrider is made by Ansel LaPier. This policy covers the Realmrider apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Realmrider has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, armory choices and whether you own the full game are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Full Realm purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase is owned.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects two devices directly over your Wi-Fi network. The game asks for Local Network permission for this. Game data (positions and moves) passes only between the two devices and is not sent to any server.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets Google see your device's IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

Realmrider collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
