## Ad-free Realm

Every world, hero, armory reward, local multiplayer and Heroic mode is free. Free play has a 15-second break after each campaign round. One in-app purchase, **Ad-free Realm**, removes it. It is a one-time purchase at the price shown in your store's currency; no subscription, nothing recurring, nothing else to buy. People who bought the earlier "Full Realm" product are ad-free already.

- **Restore on a new device or after reinstalling:** Settings › Ad-free Realm › **Restore purchase**, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
- **Refunds** are handled by the App Store or Google Play. The exact steps are in the [refund policy](legal/refunds.html).

## How to play

- Drag anywhere to steer. On Mac or with a keyboard: W A S D or the arrow keys. Enter answers whatever is asking, Escape goes back, M opens the map, P pauses.
- Leave your land and a trail follows you. Ride back into your land to claim everything inside the loop.
- A rival who touches your trail knocks you out; touch theirs to knock them out.
- Surround a rival's castle to take all of their land and coins.
- Four kinds of mission. **Most gold:** the most coins when time runs out. **Elimination:** no time limit, surround rivals' castles, last kingdom standing wins. **Defense:** every rival rides against you; keep your castle until the horn. **Capture the Flag:** carry the flag into your own land three times; most captures at the horn wins.
- Seven worlds, twelve missions each: Thornvale Meadows, Amberwood Vale, the Shattered Isles, Goldgrass Savannah, Sunspire Dunes, Frostmere Fjords and the Ashen Throne. Ten heroes and thirteen mounts are earned by playing; the armory also lets you paint your own banner.
- Replay the tutorial from Settings › Tutorial and progress.

## Local multiplayer

Two devices on the same Wi-Fi, each with Realmrider open (iPhone, iPad, Android or Mac, in any pair). One taps **Host a game**, the other **Join a game**. The host picks the bots (none to three, Squire, Knight or Warlord), the mission type, the time limit (2, 4 or 6 minutes) and the map; the guest taps Ready. If the other device does not appear, make sure both allowed Local Network access when asked (iPhone and iPad: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Realmrider). Some guest and public networks block devices from seeing each other; a phone hotspot works.

## Your data

Realmrider has no accounts and collects no personal data; everything it saves stays on your device. Details are in the [privacy policy](legal/privacy.html).

- **Reset progress** (Settings › Tutorial and progress) erases levels, records and unlocks but keeps your settings and purchase.
- **Delete my data** (Settings › Support and privacy, in game versions released after 2 November 2026) erases everything the game stored on the device, settings included, and reloads the game. Ad-free Realm can be restored from the store afterwards. In older versions, deleting the app does the same.
- Deleting the app does the same. There is no server-side account to delete; if you still want written confirmation, email us and we will send it within 30 days.

## Accessibility

The game can be played entirely with a keyboard on Mac and the web (menus, armory, settings and the purchase screen all show a visible focus ring), respects your system's Reduce Motion setting (no confetti, no animated transitions), has a performance mode for older devices, and lets you turn sound, music and vibration off separately. Text and buttons follow the WCAG AA contrast ratio. If something gets in your way, tell us and we will fix it.
