Realmrider Refund Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Version 1.0 · Support · Privacy · Terms · Cookies

Realmrider sells one thing: Ad-free Realm, a one-time purchase that removes the ad break after rounds. Every purchase is made through Apple's App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or Google Play (Android). The store takes your payment and the store issues refunds; we never hold your money or payment details, so the fastest route is always through the store. Nothing here limits the refund rights consumer law gives you where you live.

Before asking for a refund

If the purchase went through but the game still shows ads, it is almost always a restore problem, not a billing one: open Settings › Ad-free Realm › Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If that does not fix it, email us and we will sort it out the same or next day.

Apple App Store and Mac App Store

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.
  2. Choose Request a refund, pick a reason, then select Ad-free Realm under Realmrider and submit.
  3. Apple decides and usually answers within 48 hours; the money returns to the original payment method within a few days of approval.

Apple's own guide: support.apple.com/HT204084. Only Apple can refund an App Store purchase; we cannot do it for you, but we will happily confirm to Apple that you asked.

Google Play

  • Within 48 hours of buying: open play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, find the Realmrider order, choose Request a refund or Report a problem, and follow the steps. Google normally decides at once.
  • After 48 hours: email us at the address below with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). We can issue a refund from our Google Play Console and will do so for any purchase that did not work as described, and we will consider any other reasonable request.

Google's own guide: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.

Purchases made by a child

If a child bought Ad-free Realm without permission, use the store routes above and say so; Apple and Google both allow for this. To prevent it, turn on Ask to Buy (Apple Family Sharing) or purchase authentication (Google Play › Settings › Authentication).

After a refund

When the store refunds a purchase it revokes it, and the next time the game checks with the store the ad break returns. Nothing else about your game changes.
