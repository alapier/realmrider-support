# Realmrider Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE OF THE ADS RELEASE] · Version 3.0 (draft) · [Support](../index.html) · [Terms](terms.html) · [Refunds](refunds.html) · [Cookies](cookies.html)

Realmrider is made and published by Ansel LaPier ("we"). This policy covers the Realmrider game for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, and the web build. It describes only what the game actually does; it was written from the game's source code.

## The short version

- Realmrider has no accounts, no sign-in and no crash reporting. In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, the break after each round shows ads from Google AdMob; you choose whether those ads may be personalised (see Ads below). The apps also send anonymous play statistics (which missions are started, lost and won) so we can tune the difficulty (see Play statistics below).
- Everything the game saves stays on your device. We run no server and we never receive your saved progress or settings.
- The one purchase, Ad-free Realm, is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your name, email or payment details.
- You can erase everything the game stored at any time by deleting the app, or from Settings › Support and privacy › Delete my data in game versions released after 2 November 2026.

## What the game stores on your device

The game keeps one save file in the app's own local storage. It holds:

- Campaign progress: which worlds and missions you have won, your best coin total per mission, wins, streaks, knockouts and campaigns completed.
- Your armory choices (knight, armor, colour, banner, trail, aura, ship, mount) and the custom banner you painted.
- Whether you have played the tutorial, and how many tries each unwon mission has taken so far.
- Your settings: sound, music, vibration, battle feed, mountain outlines, performance mode, Heroic mode.
- Whether Ad-free Realm is owned on this device.
- The name shown to the other player in local multiplayer. The game currently fills this in itself ("An iPhone rider", "An Android rider" or "A rider"); there is no field to type your own.

The web build also keeps an offline copy of the game's own files and fonts in the browser cache so it opens without a connection.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app, or using Delete my data in Settings, removes it. Your phone or computer may include the app's data in its normal device backup (iCloud Backup on Apple devices, Google's Android backup on Android); that backup belongs to your own account and is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours.

## What leaves your device, and to whom

- **Purchases (Apple App Store, Mac App Store, Google Play).** When you buy or restore Ad-free Realm the store handles the whole transaction. The game only asks the store for the local price and learns whether the product is owned. Apple and Google process your payment under their own policies: [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Fonts (Google Fonts).** The game loads its four display fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Like any web request this sends your IP address and browser or app version to Google. Google states that the Fonts API does not set cookies and does not use these requests for advertising. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Fonts privacy notes](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy). If the fonts cannot load, the game uses built-in fonts instead.
- **Local multiplayer (your own Wi-Fi only).** When you host or join a game the two devices find each other on the local network (Bonjour/mDNS) and talk directly to each other. The host device advertises its display name; during the round the devices exchange the display names, the chosen armory loadout, positions, steering and game state. Nothing goes to any server and nothing is kept after the round. iOS asks for Local Network permission for this; the game does nothing else with that permission.

- **Ads (Google AdMob, iPhone, iPad and Android apps only).** See Ads below.
- **Play statistics (TelemetryDeck, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps only).** See Play statistics below.

That is the complete list. The game does not use your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or push notifications.

## Ads

In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, free play shows a short ad from Google AdMob after each campaign round (never in the tutorial or local multiplayer). If no ad is available, and in the Mac and web versions, the break shows the game's own card for Ad-free Realm instead. Buying Ad-free Realm removes the break entirely.

To show ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information about your device and how ads perform: your IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices.** In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form the first time it opens, and Settings › Ad-free Realm › Ad privacy choices lets you change it at any time. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks Apple's permission to track; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised and your advertising identifier is not used. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

## Play statistics

To make the missions fair, not too hard and not too easy, the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps send anonymous play statistics to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), which processes them on our behalf. The web build sends none.

What is sent: when a campaign mission starts (which mission, its game mode, and how many tries it has taken), how you were knocked off your mount in it (for example by a rival, your own trail, a beast, quicksand or lava), and when you win it. The app also sends when it is opened and how long you play, so we can see how many players come back. Each signal carries the app version, the device type and system version, and an identifier that the TelemetryDeck SDK makes on your device and scrambles (hashes) before it is sent, so neither we nor TelemetryDeck can tell who you are or link it to the advertising identifier. No name, account, email, location or advertising identifier is sent, and the statistics are not used for ads or shared with anyone else. See [TelemetryDeck's privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

## Children

Realmrider is a cartoon medieval game suitable for a general audience, and children may play it. We collect no personal information from anyone (the play statistics above are anonymous), including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR), so no parental consent is needed to play. The only purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play, where parents can use Ask to Buy, Family Sharing and purchase approval to control spending. Parents with any question can email us at the address below.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf: everything the game saves is on your own device and under your control, and the play statistics are anonymous, so they cannot be traced back to you or picked out to delete. Use Settings › Support and privacy › Delete my data to erase it, or delete the app. If you believe we hold anything about you (for example an email you sent us), write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
