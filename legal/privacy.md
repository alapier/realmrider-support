# Realmrider Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Version 2.0 · [Support](../index.html) · [Terms](terms.html) · [Refunds](refunds.html) · [Cookies](cookies.html)

Realmrider is made and published by Ansel LaPier ("we"). This policy covers the Realmrider game for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, and the web build. It describes only what the game actually does; it was written from the game's source code.

## The short version

- Realmrider has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising network and no tracking of any kind.
- Everything the game saves stays on your device. We run no server and we never receive your progress, settings or anything else.
- The one purchase, Ad-free Realm, is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your name, email or payment details.
- You can erase everything the game stored at any time by deleting the app, or from Settings › Support and privacy › Delete my data in game versions released after 2 November 2026.

## What the game stores on your device

The game keeps one save file in the app's own local storage. It holds:

- Campaign progress: which worlds and missions you have won, your best coin total per mission, wins, streaks, knockouts and campaigns completed.
- Your armory choices (knight, armor, colour, banner, trail, aura, ship, mount) and the custom banner you painted.
- Whether you have played the tutorial.
- Your settings: sound, music, vibration, battle feed, mountain outlines, performance mode, Heroic mode.
- Whether Ad-free Realm is owned on this device.
- The name shown to the other player in local multiplayer. The game currently fills this in itself ("An iPhone rider", "An Android rider" or "A rider"); there is no field to type your own.

The web build also keeps an offline copy of the game's own files and fonts in the browser cache so it opens without a connection.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app, or using Delete my data in Settings, removes it. Your phone or computer may include the app's data in its normal device backup (iCloud Backup on Apple devices, Google's Android backup on Android); that backup belongs to your own account and is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours.

## What leaves your device, and to whom

- **Purchases (Apple App Store, Mac App Store, Google Play).** When you buy or restore Ad-free Realm the store handles the whole transaction. The game only asks the store for the local price and learns whether the product is owned. Apple and Google process your payment under their own policies: [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Fonts (Google Fonts).** The game loads its four display fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Like any web request this sends your IP address and browser or app version to Google. Google states that the Fonts API does not set cookies and does not use these requests for advertising. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Fonts privacy notes](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy). If the fonts cannot load, the game uses built-in fonts instead.
- **Local multiplayer (your own Wi-Fi only).** When you host or join a game the two devices find each other on the local network (Bonjour/mDNS) and talk directly to each other. The host device advertises its display name; during the round the devices exchange the display names, the chosen armory loadout, positions, steering and game state. Nothing goes to any server and nothing is kept after the round. iOS asks for Local Network permission for this; the game does nothing else with that permission.

That is the complete list. The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising identifier or push notifications.

## Ads

Free play includes a 15-second break after each campaign round. Today that break shows the game's own card for Ad-free Realm; no third-party advertising code is in the game and nothing about you is sent anywhere. If we ever add an advertising network, we will update this policy, the store listings and the game before that version ships, and ads shown to anyone who may be a child will be non-personalised.

## Children

Realmrider is a cartoon medieval game suitable for a general audience, and children may play it. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR), so no parental consent is needed to play. The only purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play, where parents can use Ask to Buy, Family Sharing and purchase approval to control spending. Parents with any question can email us at the address below.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf: every piece of data the game creates is on your own device and under your control. Use Settings › Support and privacy › Delete my data to erase it, or delete the app. If you believe we hold anything about you (for example an email you sent us), write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

When this policy changes we post the new version here with a new effective date and version number. Version 1.0 was published on 27 October 2026.
